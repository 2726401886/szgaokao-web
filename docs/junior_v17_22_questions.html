Part C · 对话听力（核心陷阱）

v17C01 陷阱：张冠李戴
W: I bought a purple notebook yesterday.
M: Really? I have a blue one.
What color is the boy's notebook?
  1. Purple
  2. ✅ Blue
  3. Red
🔎 男孩说 I have a blue one，别被女孩的 purple 干扰。
💡 设题陷阱：张冠李戴。
v17C02 陷阱：数字计算
M: I had twelve pencils, but I lost two this morning.
W: So you have ten now?
How many pencils does Tom have now?
  1. 12
  2. 2
  3. ✅ 10
🔎 12-2=10，录音不念结果，需自己算。
💡 设题陷阱：数字计算。
v17C03 陷阱：转折后取义
W: Let's buy some strawberries at the market.
M: But it's closed. We can go to the supermarket.

  1. Market
  2. ✅ Supermarket
  3. School
🔎 but 之后才是最终决定（去超市）。
💡 设题陷阱：转折后取义。
v17C04 陷阱：态度推断

  3. Afraid
🔎 amazing / love 表惊喜喜爱。
💡 设题陷阱：态度推断。
v17C05 陷阱：地点身份
